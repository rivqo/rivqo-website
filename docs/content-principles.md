# Content principles

Messaging should help a project-based operator recognise an expensive operational problem and see a credible path to improvement. Do not invent proof or final marketing copy until it is confirmed.

## Route map

| Path          | Purpose                                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/`           | Recognise the problem, see the method in brief, and start a conversation |
| `/solutions`  | Understand the workflows Rivqo may improve                               |
| `/method`     | Understand diagnostic-to-rollout work and whether Rivqo is a fit         |
| `/industries` | See whether the company’s operating reality is in scope                  |
| `/about`      | Understand why Rivqo exists and what it will not claim                   |
| `/contact`    | Send an enquiry about one operational problem                            |
| `/privacy`    | How enquiry and analytics information is handled                         |

Do not duplicate the homepage verbatim on supporting pages. Each page should add depth.

## What the site must communicate

1. Who Rivqo serves
2. The expensive operational problems those companies face
3. What Rivqo can improve
4. How Rivqo delivers improvement
5. Why prospective clients should trust Rivqo
6. How a company can begin—today, through the contact form or email; later, by a paid diagnostic

## Voice

- Lead with business outcomes, not technology.
- Use concrete operational language.
- Speak directly to project-based companies.
- Keep headings concise and specific.
- Explain technical capability only after establishing business relevance.

## Preferred language

| Prefer                      | Avoid                  |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| procurement control         | digital transformation |
| reliable project visibility | advanced analytics     |
| connected workflows         | seamless ecosystem     |

## Do not

- Do not lead with AI.
- Do not call Rivqo an ERP.
- Do not claim guaranteed savings.
- Do not fabricate proof, client logos, testimonials, results or statistics.
- Do not invent phone numbers, addresses, emails, booking URLs or client information.
- Do not publish contact details that have not been confirmed.
- Do not present an inactive form, a fake success state or “coming soon”.

## Approved CTA language

- Primary: `Start a conversation` → `/contact`
- Secondary: `See how Rivqo works` → `/#method` on the homepage, `/method` on supporting pages
- Contact action: `Start a conversation` on `/contact`, with `mailto:ola@rivqo.com` as the fallback

Do not alternate these with “Book a call”, “Contact us”, “Request a demo” or generic “Start”.

## Heading story on the homepage

The major headings should read, in order:

1. The desired operating outcome
2. The current information problem
3. Different stakeholder questions
4. The workflows Rivqo holds together
5. Fragmented tools versus a connected trail
6. Diagnose, prove, then expand
7. The industries Rivqo serves
8. Why Rivqo is credible
9. The starting action

Use “operational control”, “reliable operating view” and “connected workflow” once, where they earn their keep. Do not repeat them in every section.

## Credibility and proof

The About page may state firsthand experience, technical depth, diagnostic-first delivery and honest scope. Case studies render only from verified entries in `src/data/homepage.ts`. An empty list stays hidden.

Approved proof boundary:

`Rivqo’s public case-study library is still being developed. We do not publish client names, results or claims without verification and permission.`

## Contact approach

`/contact` is the public enquiry form. Email remains the fallback if delivery fails or the visitor prefers to write directly.

## Remaining confirmation

- Phone number and physical address
- Booking link
- Founder names and biographies
- Verified case studies
- Counsel review of `/privacy`
